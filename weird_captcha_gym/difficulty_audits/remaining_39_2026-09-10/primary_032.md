# Hearthlift Courier — primary assessment

Decision: revise the task/profile claims before calibrating this ladder; no numerical relabel is justified yet. The parent read the complete controls, generator, browser JavaScript/CSS, grader, task verifier, scripted solver and task/environment specifications. Existing L4/Full is retained as an unconfirmed label, not endorsed on the reviewer's prepare/return/carry rationale.

An additional parent finding changes that rationale: the advertised preparation and return trip are unnecessary. The player can orbit, pick up cargo immediately, climb each unmoved helper crate, take three eastward steps and the generated turn at each stage, then drop at the hearth. Climb permits carried cargo, and normal movement permits a one-voxel change in either direction. It is therefore possible to step down from the initial helper and then up the next ledge without pushing the helper into a new supporting position. See generator lines 228–270 and 323–357; browser lines 34–75; grader lines 57–106.

`hearthlift_carry_first_checks.json` records passing independent replays of that route for all 30 generated difficulty/mode/seed configurations, with every helper and decoy box unchanged. Separate L4 seed-1 Full and Simplified browser diagnostics exercise that route through existing controls and record browser/server/direct/exported-verifier results. These are structural counterexamples to a required preparation/return dependency, not screenshot-agent performance measurements. Do not repair this by adding an arbitrary push or trip quota: if support placement is intended to matter, the movement and generated geometry must make it necessary.

The review's parameter findings are also supported, with precise scope. `stack_height` is syntactically consumed in the decoy-height modulus, but every implemented profile's decoy indices stay below it, so that configured distinction does not take effect. `cargo_detour` selects one of two equally adjacent same-height pickup cells; it does not add a longer detour. Side crates are explicitly labelled DECOY in the renderer, so they can add clutter but should not be credited as unknown route alternatives. See generator lines 274–297, controls lines 5–34, and browser lines 158–175.

The camera-event and action-count checks in the grader are not difficulty evidence. In particular, its `push_or_climb` counter also increments ordinary movement. The new counterexample is a legal visible-control route with actual climbing, not a forged transcript or a claim based only on that counter.
