# Pilot: final primary assessment

The ten pilot decisions are four provisional keeps and six profile/claim revisions; all numeric baselines remain unchanged. The original independent reports returned six keeps and four revisions. The parent additionally puts Cloudstep Caddie and Lanternfin Dive into revision because their active/rendered mechanics do not support parts of the profile descriptions.

The parent read each complete generator, assigned browser mechanic and CSS, grader, task verifier, solver and control/task specifications, plus the implementations of five actual approved anchors: Isometric Voxel Extraction Mine L1, Cursor-Controlled Constellation Hunt L2, Gyroscopic Tilt Board L3, Blind Dice Courier L4 and Exact-Change Candy Cascade L5. Relevant shared dispatch paths were followed; this is not a claim to have read every unrelated function in monolithic helpers.
